Scanner and printer help
  1. Camera: tap Scan QR / barcode, allow camera access, and hold a product label in view. A unique in-stock match is added to the current bill automatically.
  2. Handheld scanner: pair a Bluetooth/USB keyboard-style barcode scanner, tap the search box, and scan. The scanner must send Enter after the code. You can also type the code and press Enter.
  3. Printer: connect the receipt printer using your device's USB, Wi-Fi, or Bluetooth printer setup. On Android, install or enable its print service if required; then choose the printer from the browser's Print dialog.
  4. After a sale: select Print Bill on the completed receipt. The browser print dialog lets you select your printer and paper size. For thermal printers, choose 80 mm paper, minimum margins and no headers/footers.
