PRINTER TEST — NOT AN INVOICE

Receipt printer connection check

If this prints clearly, your printer is ready for bills.

Choose your receipt printer in the browser print dialog. For an 80 mm thermal printer, select 80 mm receipt paper, margins None or Minimum, scale 100%, and disable headers/footers. For an Android tablet, pair or install the printer in Android settings first. Printer availability depends on the device and browser.
