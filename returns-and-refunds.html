CUSTOMER HELP

Returns & order concerns

We do not accept change-of-mind returns. Please check the design, size, colour, length and other selected options before placing your order. Sales are final for change of mind. This does not limit any rights you have under applicable consumer law, including for damaged, defective or incorrect items.

Damaged, defective or incorrect items

If an item arrives damaged, is defective, or does not match your order, contact Rainbow Gold Covering promptly with your order number and details of the issue. Keep the packaging and clear photos so the store can review the concern and explain the appropriate next steps. The no-change-of-mind-returns policy does not remove your applicable consumer rights.

How to request help

  1. Contact the store with your order number, product code, and a description of the issue. Include clear photos of any damage, defect, or difference from your order.
  2. Ask the store to confirm the next steps, whether the item needs to be returned, the return address, and any shipping arrangements before sending it back.
  3. Keep the item, packaging, payment receipt, and any return-tracking details while the request is being reviewed.

Refund and exchange arrangements

For a resolution involving a refund or replacement, contact the store to confirm the amount or replacement item, the refund method, any return-shipping arrangements, and the expected processing time for your case. Ask for these details in writing. This policy does not promise a change-of-mind exchange; contact the store before ordering if you need to confirm exchange arrangements.

Questions about a request

Contact the store through the same phone or WhatsApp details with your order number and previous correspondence so staff can follow up.
