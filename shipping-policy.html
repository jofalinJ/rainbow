CUSTOMER HELP

Shipping & order tracking

Find delivery charges and tracking information below. Before placing an order, contact the store to confirm delivery availability for your PIN code and the expected dispatch and arrival dates, especially if you need jewellery for a particular occasion.

How orders are sent

Rainbow Gold Covering currently uses The Professional Couriers for applicable shipments. After dispatch, the store can provide your consignment or AWB number. View your saved order status through the account menu or track the parcel on the courier's official website.

Delivery charges

The cart calculates a delivery fee from the selected state and displays it before payment. The published checkout currently calculates ₹50 within Tamil Nadu and ₹100 for other supported Indian states and union territories; review the displayed total before placing your order.

Delivery dates and store pickup

Contact the store for an estimate for your address before paying. If you would prefer store pickup, ask which branch can arrange it and when the jewellery will be ready. Do not travel to collect an order until the store confirms the arrangement.

Delayed, missing or damaged parcels

Contact Rainbow Gold Covering with your order number, consignment or AWB number, and a description of the issue. For a damaged parcel, keep the packaging and clear photos. Report damaged, defective or incorrect items using the same contact details. The store can review the shipment with you and explain the next steps; see the returns and order concerns information for what to include when reporting an issue.
